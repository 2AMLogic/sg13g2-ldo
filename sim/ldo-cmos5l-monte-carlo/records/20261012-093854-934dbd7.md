**not** support a 3σ (99.73 %) or tighter row-2 yield.

## Comparison with record 20261009-003009-3ee8306 (what changed, what did not)

That record ran the **five-port pre-EN** core (`c6e630e9…`, before #67 and
#89) on the **old** pin `607e18d`. This one runs the current EN core on the
new pin. Seeds are shared draw for draw, so the two can be paired:

- Every one of the 2752 rows has the same seeds and the same pass/fail
  status in both records.
- Per-draw `vout_v` differences: **0 or 1 nV** (the 10th printed digit) in
  every request and population at −40 °C and 27 °C (tt, ss_rbcs, fs_rbcs,
  attribution, negative control, reproduction, probe); up to **1.75 µV**
  (ff_rwcs/125 °C) and **0.64 µV** (sf_rwcs/125 °C) at 125 °C. Every
  population statistic in the table above equals the earlier record's to
  the printed digits except the ff/sf 125 °C means and extremes (≤ 2 µV).
- **Attribution of the difference.** The staged model files are
  byte-identical between the two records (13 of 13 in every request) and the
  OSDI binaries are the same fleet-image binaries, so the PDK re-stamp
  changed no model input to this campaign. The remaining µV-level shift is
  therefore the design change (the #67 `EN` devices and the #89 pull-down,
  which are off but present, and whose leakage is largest at 125 °C), not
  the PDK. This is an inference from the identical model inputs, not a
  separate design-held-constant run: the five-port deck cannot elaborate
  against the current core, and a pre-EN core on the new pin was not run.
- The set of draws the runner flagged as recovered solves **did** change
  (368 of 2000 main draws now, 482 before; see "Convergence"), which also
  points at the netlist rather than the models.

## Convergence

No draw in any request errored, came back null or was graded inconclusive
(`crosscheck.json` → `accounting`). The runner attached a recovered-solve
`warning: singular_matrix` diagnostic to **368 of 2000** main draws
(fs_rbcs/−40 °C 190, ss_rbcs/−40 °C 89, tt 57, sf_rwcs 20, ff_rwcs 12),
71/400 negative-control draws, 31/300 attribution draws and 6/50
reproduction draws. In the 50 retained reproduction logs, 36 solves report
`Dynamic gmin stepping completed`. The runner's klt 0.5.0 cannot grade these
as inconclusive (`options.fail_on_diagnostic` is newer, klayout-tools#2901),
so they are **included** in the yield, and their effect is stated:

| Population | recovered / clean | mean, recovered vs clean (V) | closest margin, recovered vs clean (mV) | zero-failure lower bound on the clean draws alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tt / 27 °C | 57 / 343 | 1.80406 vs 1.80067 | 8.17 vs 3.79 | 0.9893 |
| ss_rbcs / −40 °C | 89 / 311 | 1.79986 vs 1.80083 | 12.33 vs 4.13 | 0.9882 |
| ff_rwcs / 125 °C | 12 / 388 | 1.80096 vs 1.80053 | 22.29 vs 3.91 | 0.9905 |
| sf_rwcs / 125 °C | 20 / 380 | 1.80351 vs 1.80085 | 14.43 vs 9.50 | 0.9903 |
| fs_rbcs / −40 °C | 190 / 210 | 1.79939 vs 1.80009 | **1.31** vs 12.50 | 0.9826 |

As before, the recovered draws are not an outlier class (means within
3.4 mV of the clean draws) but include the campaign's closest draw
(fs_rbcs draw 329). **The planning-target pass depends on counting them.** A
reviewer who does not trust gmin-stepped operating points should read tt,
ss_rbcs and fs_rbcs as "≥ 98.3–98.9 %" rather than "≥ 99.08 %".

## What this record does not show

- **Row 2 is not ratified** by this record and its scope wording is not
  decided by it (two-key review and a decision-record update, not this PR).
- **Reference error is excluded.** A real 0.9 V reference's spread must be
  combined with this before reading it against a system ±2 % budget.
- **Not covered:** global/process statistical sampling (five named corners
  stand in for it); `Vin`/load variation (rows 1/5, #55); `Cc` (no mismatch
  model; open at DC); the disabled state (`EN` is tied to `VIN`); post-layout
  effects (item 7); ppm-level tails.
- **Row 8** (current-limit window) has no campaign: there is no limiter (#63).
- **T1 item 6 is left uncited** in `signoff/sg13g2-ldo.json`, for the reasons
  the earlier record's "Signoff grading check" gives (klayout-tools#2908:
  one Open row's evidence would mark the whole item met while row 8 has
  none). That grading experiment was not repeated here.
