**Reading it honestly.** No draw left the window: 2000/2000 pass. The
closest was 1.765307 V (fs_rbcs/−40 °C, draw 329), 1.3 mV inside the lower
limit. With N = 400 and zero failures, the strongest defensible statement
per population is "**at least 99.08 % at 95 % confidence**", not 100 %.
That clears the planning target 0.99, a sample-size design input and not a
ratified yield (README "Seeds, sample size, convergence"). The normal fit
(Anderson-Darling consistent in all five) extrapolates **≈ 120–790 ppm**
fallout per population, with the mean 3.27–3.78 σ from the nearest limit.
So the evidence supports a 99 %-class claim. It does **not** support a 3σ
(99.73 %) or tighter row-2 yield. Whatever yield the ratified row ends up
requiring decides whether this design meets it, and a ppm-level target
would need importance sampling or a larger campaign.

## Cross-checks (`20261009-003009-3ee8306/crosscheck.json`)

- **Sampler negative control** (attribution, `tt_nom`: mismatch off, 100
  distinct seeds): all 100 values are **identical**, 1.800306041 V. The
  committed corner record gives 1.80030601 V (tt/27 °C/res_typ
  `vout_no_load_v`), a difference of 31 nV. The sampler injects no variation

  `klt yield` misreports this degenerate set as "normality rejected, Cpk

  `crosscheck.json`.
- **Attribution at tt/27 °C** (N = 100 each): MOS mismatch only σ = 9.908 mV;
  `rhigh` mismatch only σ = 4.540 mV; RSS 10.90 mV against 10.66 mV for the
  full-mismatch tt population (ratio 1.02). The MOS share of the variance is
  **83 %**. That share covers the error-amplifier pair, both mirrors, the
  bias leg and `Mpass`, all sampled together; per-device attribution is not
  done here. The divider ratio is the remaining 17 %. DR-0007's "`Mpass` is
  not accuracy-relevant" is therefore *not* contradicted, but it is not

## Convergence

No draw in any request errored, came back null or was graded inconclusive
(`crosscheck.json` → `accounting`: draws = usable for every population). A
solve that needed help is disclosed rather than dropped. The runner attached
a recovered-solve `warning: singular_matrix` diagnostic to **482 of 2000**
main draws (ss_rbcs/−40 °C 201, fs_rbcs/−40 °C 173, tt 97, ff_rwcs 7,
sf_rwcs 4), 97/400 negative-control draws, 42/300 attribution draws and
13/50 reproduction draws. In the 50 retained reproduction logs
(`corners/20261009-003009-3ee8306/`), 34 solves used ngspice's dynamic gmin
stepping, and all 34 completed. Every `singular matrix` warning (at the
nulling resistor's internal node `xtop.xamp.xrz.nr1#i1`) and every r3_cmc
`vmax` warning appears only inside a gmin-stepping solve, i.e. during
intermediate iterations. None appears in a direct solve. `XRz` carries no DC
current (`Cc` is open at DC), which explains the near-singular node.
Recovered solves are **included** in the yield. The runner's klt 0.5.0
cannot grade them as inconclusive (`options.fail_on_diagnostic` is newer).
Their effect, stated rather than assumed:

| Population | recovered / clean | mean, recovered vs clean (V) | closest margin, recovered vs clean (mV) | zero-failure lower bound on the clean draws alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tt / 27 °C | 97 / 303 | 1.80176 vs 1.80096 | 6.80 vs 3.79 | 0.9879 |
| ss_rbcs / −40 °C | 201 / 199 | 1.80027 vs 1.80096 | 4.13 vs 9.53 | 0.9816 |

The recovered draws are not an outlier class: their means sit within 1 mV
of the clean draws. They do include the campaign's closest draw (fs_rbcs
draw 329). Every value, recovered or clean, is inside the window. But
**the planning-target pass depends on counting them**. Dropping them would
leave 199–396 draws per population, and the zero-failure lower bound would
fall below 0.99 for tt, ss_rbcs and fs_rbcs. A reviewer who does not trust
gmin-stepped operating points should read those three populations as
"≥ 98.2–98.8 %" rather than "≥ 99.08 %". Re-grading with
`fail_on_diagnostic` needs a fleet runner newer than klt 0.5.0
(klayout-tools#2901).
