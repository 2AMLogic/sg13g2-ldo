## f_ug vs Cc: the slope and what it can say (AC 5)

All three runs have the SAME number of 0 dB crossings, so every f_ug is the same branch's crossing and the slope is a single-branch measurement.

Least-squares log-log slope of f_ug against Cc/Cc_nom over the three labels: **0.002539** (i.e. f_ug proportional to Cc^-0.002539).

DR-0015's own framing (its class-2 section) names the two limiting readings: f_ug proportional to Cc^-1 would point at a pole set by the compensation network itself (a Cc-side pole moving with the cap), while f_ug insensitive to Cc (slope near 0) would point at a pole fixed by a large node capacitance independent of Cc -- the fixed-gate-side reading. Intermediate exponents mean the crossover sits between those idealisations; the attribution call is made in the DR-0015 addendum, not here.
